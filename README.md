# Study Habits Analyzer

A small tool that gives students feedback on their study habits, based on general findings from published education research. Works for students at any university.

**Live demo:** https://euuebx.github.io/study-habits-analyser/

## What it does

The student answers 11 short questions about their study habits. The app scores each answer and shows:
- An overall verdict
- Their strongest habits
- Their weakest habits
- One specific thing to try this week

## Why it exists

Most student success tools predict grades from private data, which needs ethics approval and only works at one institution. This tool does the opposite: it uses public research findings to give anyone useful feedback, with no data collection.

## Honest note on the scores

The scores in the app are **illustrative**. They reflect general trends in published education research (spaced practice, sleep, active recall, attendance, task switching, etc.) but they are not a fitted model on real data.
